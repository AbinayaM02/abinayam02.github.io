<b> Abstract </b>

NL-Augmenter is a collaborative repository of natural language transformations. It collects transformations and filters contributed by many authors, so that language models can be evaluated for robustness against realistic variation in their inputs rather than on clean test data alone. The resulting suite underpins the task-sensitive augmentation framework published as NL-Augmenter.
